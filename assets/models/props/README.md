# Scripted props

Modelled for Tiptoe by a script, `tools/blender/props.py` (`<python with bpy> tools/blender/props.py assets/models/props`); change the script and rerun it rather than editing these files. Metres, Y up. Wall props have their back on z = 0, face +Z, are centred on x and sit on y = 0. Floor props stand on y = 0, centred on x and z. Flat-colour materials only, shared by name (`metal_light`, `metal_grey`, `metal_dark`, `dark`, `glass`, `red_led` (emissive), `red`, `yellow`, `brass`, `fabric_dark`, `fabric_light`).

- `security_camera.glb`: wall CCTV camera (280 tris). `mount` is the wall plate and arm. `head` is the body, lens and red LED, with its origin on the pivot at the top of the arm (0, 0.09, 0.135); rotate it about Y to pan.
- `safe.glb`: floor safe, 0.6 x 0.7 x 0.6 m, with a shelf inside (464 tris). `body`. `door` has its origin on the hinge edge (0.26, 0.37, 0.24); a positive Y rotation opens it. Its children are `dial` (origin at the dial centre, turn it about local Z, the door's normal) and `handle` (a three-spoke wheel, origin at its hub, also turns about local Z).
- `wall_vent.glb`: wall air vent, 0.6 x 0.4 m (234 tris). `frame`. `cover` is the lift-off louvred grille, origin in the middle of the opening on the wall (0, 0.2, 0). `duct` is a dark liner going 0.5 m back into the wall (z down to -0.5), with its faces pointing inward. The wall needs a hole behind the 0.5 x 0.3 m opening for the duct to show.
- `fuse_box.glb`: grey wall fuse box, 0.4 x 0.5 x 0.15 m (332 tris). `box` holds a row of eight fuse switches. Its child `breaker` is the main switch lever, origin at its pivot (0, 0.365, 0.05); rotation.x 0 is up (on) and about +70 degrees is down (off). `door` has its origin on the hinge (0.2, 0.25, 0.13); a positive Y rotation opens it.
- `doorbell.glb`: front-door bell, 0.07 x 0.11 m brass plate (142 tris). `plate`. `button` has its origin on the bezel face (0, 0.045, 0.018) and pushes in along -Z by up to about 6 mm.
- `gloves.glb`: first-person gloves (500 tris each), charcoal with lighter cuffs, in a relaxed half-open pose. `glove_left` sits at (-0.12, 0, 0) and `glove_right` at (0.12, 0, 0), each with its origin at the wrist. Fingers point to -Z (Godot's forward), backs of the hands face up (+Y) and the thumbs point inward.
